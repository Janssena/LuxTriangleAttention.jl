Kernel operands

What an attention kernel reads, for anyone writing one or reading the built-in ones. The operands are handed over in place: nothing is permuted, padded or copied beyond what this page says.

Notation: hd is the head dimension, H the heads, Nq the attention length (queries = keys), Nj the outer extent (== Nq for triangle attention, 1 for a plain [hd, H, N, B] sequence), B the batch and T the storage type (Float32, Float16 or BFloat16; BFloat16s.jl's, the type Metal.jl and Reactant use). Every array is column-major, first axis fastest.

operandshapeeltypenotes
q, k, v, dO, o[hd, H, Nq, Nj, B]Toffset d + hd·(h + H·(j + Nq·(i + Nj·b)))
lse, delta[1, Nq, H, Nj, B]Float32offset j + Nq·(h + H·(i + Nj·b)); natural-log logsumexp, and Σ_d o·dO
bias[Nk, Nq, H, B]Tkeys fastest; shared across Nj
dbias[Nk, Nq, H, B]Float32accumulate into a zeroed OutArray; sum over Nj
mask[Nk, Nj, B]Bool (1 byte)true = DROP the key: a key mask per outer index. The layer's mask is a KEEP mask; _sdpa_prep negates it

An absent bias or mask becomes a 1-element placeholder when skip = true, which is only for a kernel whose binaries are specialised never to read it (the built-in ones). With skip = false it becomes a zero bias and an all-live mask at full size, which is what trifast takes.

Two things that are silent when wrong:

  • Batch and head are not adjacent in the [hd, H, Nq, Nj, B] arrays or in lse and delta. A kernel that walks a combined bh = b·H + h index with one stride is right only for B = 1. The bias is (b, h)-adjacent.
  • Each array and each scalar is its own Metal buffer slot. A launch binds at most 31 of them, so derive strides in the kernel rather than passing one per dimension.

The built-in argument lists

Written once, in src/kernels/stage_args.jl (stage_args, used by the op and by the tuner alike), and pinned by test/kernels/stage_args.test.jl. Each launch's threadgroups are (cld(Nq, rows), Nj, H·B) with rows the binary's tile[1].

:default   fwd  o lse | q k v bias_t mask | scale neg_inf n nouter nh mheads
           dq   dq dbias_t | q k v bias_t mask dO lse delta | scale n nouter nh mheads
           dk   dk | q k v bias mask dO lse delta | scale n nouter nh mheads
           dv   dv | q k bias mask dO lse | scale n nouter nh mheads
:flash_mma fwd  o lse | q k v bias mask | scale n nouter nh mheads
           dkv  dk dv dbias | q k v bias mask dO lse delta | scale n nouter nh mheads
           dq   dq | q k v bias mask dO lse delta | scale n nouter nh mheads

Outputs come first, then the inputs, then the scalars: scale = 1/√hd (Float32), n = Nq, nouter = Nj, nh = H, mheads = H (all Int32). bias_t and dbias_t are the pair bias with its two pair axes swapped ([Nq, Nk, H·B], queries fastest), which a kernel with one thread per query reads coalesced.

Variants

The built-in kernels key on (dtype, dim, N, hasbias, hasmask); see Fused attention kernels.

Internal

The helper functions that build these operands (_sdpa_prep, _finish, _full, _res, ...) are internal. They are documented in their docstrings and are the quickest route to correct operands, but they may change between versions.
